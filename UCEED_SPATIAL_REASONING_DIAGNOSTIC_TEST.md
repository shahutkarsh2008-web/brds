# UCEED Spatial Reasoning Diagnostic Test

**Source:** UCEED Part A Spatial Reasoning Worksheet  
**Topics:** 2D to 3D Visualization, Rotation, Reflection, Mirror/Water Images, Symmetry, Top/Front/Side Views  
**Format:** 18 questions | 30 minutes | 36 marks  
**Marking:** +2 correct, -0.5 incorrect, 0 unattempted

## Instructions

Attempt without the worksheet answer key. Draw rough diagrams where needed. Record confidence (High/Medium/Low) for every answer.

## Questions

1. In a cube net, faces two positions apart in a straight strip become: (A) adjacent (B) opposite (C) coincident (D) parallel only.
2. A 2D rectangle is extruded perpendicular to its plane. The resulting solid is: (A) cylinder (B) cone (C) cuboid (D) sphere.
3. A figure is rotated 90° clockwise and then 270° clockwise. Net result: (A) 0° (B) 90° clockwise (C) 180° (D) 270° anticlockwise.
4. Which operation changes handedness? (A) 90° rotation (B) 180° rotation (C) reflection (D) translation.
5. Reflection in a vertical axis exchanges: (A) top/bottom (B) left/right (C) front/back (D) diagonals only.
6. Reflection in a horizontal axis followed by a vertical-axis reflection is equivalent to: (A) translation (B) 90° rotation (C) 180° rotation (D) no change.
7. A vertical mirror is placed to the right of a word. The image has: (A) left-right reversal (B) top-bottom reversal (C) no reversal (D) 90° rotation.
8. A water image below a figure reverses: (A) left/right only (B) top/bottom only (C) both axes (D) neither.
9. Two mirrors at 90° form how many images of an object between them? (A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4.
10. Two mirrors at 60° form how many images? (A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6.
11. A regular hexagon has how many lines of symmetry? (A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6.
12. A non-square rectangle has rotational symmetry of order: (A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4.
13. A rhombus (not square) has how many lines of symmetry? (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 4.
14. A solid cylinder standing upright appears from the top as: (A) circle (B) rectangle (C) triangle (D) semicircle.
15. A cuboid viewed from the front generally shows: (A) a rectangle (B) a circle (C) a triangle (D) a hexagon.
16. A cube sliced parallel to one face produces a: (A) square (B) circle (C) pentagon (D) ellipse.
17. A 3x3x3 cube has how many completely hidden unit cubes? (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 8 (D) 9.
18. A pinwheel can have rotational symmetry but: (A) must have four lines of symmetry (B) can have no line of symmetry (C) cannot rotate (D) is always mirror-symmetric.
